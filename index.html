Rates, tax and budget settings
How the maths works
  • Hours you enter are on-site hours, including your unpaid break. The app takes the break off before working out your pay.
  • Weeknight: paid hours up to the first overtime point are paid at base + night loading. Hours between the first and second points are paid at the first multiplier × base + loading. Hours after the second point are paid at the second multiplier × base + loading.
  • Weekend: any shift that starts on a Saturday or Sunday is paid at the weekend multiplier × base for every paid hour, however long the shift is. Union shifts on a weekend are paid at the union rate × the union night multiplier.
  • Day shift: paid at the base rate with no night loading, using the same overtime points and multipliers as nights.
  • Dayshift on Nights: the first set number of hours are paid at the first multiplier × base, and the rest at the second multiplier × base. There is no night loading and there are no overtime tiers.
  • Union day: paid at the union rate up to the union day overtime point, then at the union day multiplier. Union night is always paid at the union night multiplier.
  • Union site: the union rate × the union multiplier, plus the site allowance you enter for that day, for every paid hour.
  • Public holiday: the public holiday multiplier × base + loading for every paid hour, plus any bonus hours at base.
  • Public holiday off: a public holiday you don't work is paid at base + night loading for the paid hours, with no bonus hours.
  • Union public holiday: the union rate × the union public holiday multiplier, plus the site allowance, for every paid hour, plus the public holiday bonus hours at base. On a public holiday, the choices are Off, Public holiday off, Public holiday and Union public holiday.
  • Hyrail is paid for each paid hour, on-call is paid per day, and living away from home is paid per night. Set any of these to 0 if you don't get them.
  • Minimum hours: if you set guaranteed weekly hours and turn on the top-up, any shortfall in a week is topped up at base + loading.
  • Tax is an estimate of PAYG withholding at 2026–27 Australian resident rates, plus the 2% Medicare levy, treating each week as 1/52 of a year. Your payslip may differ slightly.
  • Victorian public holidays for 2026 and 2027 are marked. Melbourne Cup Day applies to metro Melbourne only.
  • Your privacy: nothing you enter leaves this device. There is no account and no server, so nobody else can see your figures. Clearing your browser data deletes your log, so copy a backup every now and then.
