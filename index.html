Rates, tax and budget settings
How the maths works
  • Hours you enter are on-site hours, including your unpaid break. The app takes the break off before working out pay.
  • Weeknight: paid hours up to the first overtime point are base + night loading. Hours between the first and second points are the first multiplier x base + loading. Hours after the second point are the second multiplier x base + loading.
  • Weekend: the weekend multiplier x base for every paid hour. Turn on "Add night loading on weekends" if your award adds it.
  • Public holiday: the public holiday multiplier x base + loading for every paid hour, plus any bonus hours at base.
  • Union site: the union rate x the union multiplier, plus the site allowance you enter on the day, for every paid hour.
  • Hyrail is paid per paid hour. On-call is paid per day. Living away from home is paid per night. Set these to 0 if you don't get them.
  • Minimum hours: if you set guaranteed weekly hours and turn on the top-up, a short week is topped up at base + loading.
  • Tax is an estimate of PAYG withholding at 2026-27 Australian resident rates plus the 2% Medicare levy, treating the week as 1/52 of a year. Your payslip may differ slightly.
  • Victorian public holidays for 2026 and 2027 are marked. Melbourne Cup Day is metro Melbourne only.
  • Your privacy: nothing you enter leaves this device. There is no account and no server, and nobody else can see your figures. Clearing your browser data deletes your log, so copy a backup now and then.
